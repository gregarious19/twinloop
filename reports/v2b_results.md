Counts differ because lesions are detected on the radius profile, and r_eq ≠ r_ins (different ds = 1 − r/r_ref at every point), so lesions cross the 0.25 / 0.5 / 0.7 thresholds. The jump in mild lesions under area_eq is large (about 3x); its cause was not investigated here (a noisier r_eq profile from CPR cross-sections near junctions or neighbouring vessels is one candidate), so lesion counts and bins are not comparable across modes.

## 5. Caveats

- Exploratory: n = 20 cases, all seen in S16/S18/S19; confirmatory tests need a new pre-selected stenosis-enriched set.
- Few severe lesions; bin statistics rest on small n (see the `n` columns).
- The metrics are relative to GT-mask lesions, not to invasive angiography.
- FFR values are unvalidated (no invasive-FFR data) and not clinically accurate.
- Non-repair rows run without TotalSegmentator and with diameter-law flow; repair rows use anatomy + flow mode `auto` as in S16, so FFR differs in flow model between those groups.
